Heliotrope sun-mapped garden planning · on real satellite imagery

1Find your yard. Search your address — the map flies to a satellite view of your actual garden, and the sun math locks to your latitude.
2Trace what's there. Drag out beds over the soil you can see, then outline trees, buildings and fences with their real heights — they will cast the shadows.
3Plant by the light. Scrub through the day and the seasons, switch on the sun-hours map, and Heliotrope flags every plant that won't get the light it needs.
